About Rizzq Farm

Rizzq Farm is based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. We follow ethical farming practices, provide quality feed, and ensure regular veterinary care for all livestock. Our goal is to supply healthy animals for dairy, breeding and smallholders across Tamil Nadu and neighbouring states.

Our Services

  • Sale of dairy and breeding animals
  • Transport & delivery coordination
  • Breeding consultation and support
  • On-site visits & farm tours (by appointment)

Farm Practices

We emphasize natural feed, clean sheds, and humane handling. All animals receive required vaccinations and health checks before sale.
